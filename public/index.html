Devil's Den Play
Inspired by The Devil’s Plan

Outsmart your friends,
one game at a time.

Devil’s Den is a game night of quick brain games you play with friends online. One person opens a room and picks the games. Everyone joins from their own phone, plays a few fast rounds of each, and earns pieces. Most pieces at the end of the night wins.

Host a night

No accounts, no downloads. 2–10 players on any phone or laptop.

1

Open a room

The host gets a 4-letter code to share in the group chat or on a Discord call.

2

Pick the games

Choose one or more games, each with its own difficulty and number of rounds.

3

Play for pieces

Win a game to earn pieces. The night goes to whoever has the most.
